Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 2026

AI FLOW respects user privacy and provides a way for users to request deletion of their data.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, please email us at: baskinltd@yahoo.com

Please include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if used with AI FLOW
  • The platform where you interacted with AI FLOW, such as website, Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp

What We Delete

Upon a valid request, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your interaction, including:

  • Lead information
  • Message history where applicable
  • Booking information where applicable
  • Contact information

Processing Time

We aim to process valid deletion requests within a reasonable time, unless retention is required for legal, security, or operational reasons.
